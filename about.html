About Me

Educational entrepreneur on a mission to make learning accessible, engaging and effective for everyone

My Story

Hello! I'm Linda Bernard

For over 25 years, I've been asking the same question in every classroom, edtech company, and country I've worked in: How do we reach every learner?

That question has taken me around the world, leading schools across multiple continents, building curriculum, mentoring teachers, and eventually co-founding companies. Along the way, I discovered that technology, when designed with care and grounded in how people actually learn, can do something remarkable: it can make great teaching scale without losing its soul.

Today, I'm COO and Chief Learning Officer at UltiSim Education, where we're pioneering digital twin technology for learning. I co-founded QuestSim, a narrative-based 3D learning adventure platform that immerses students in story-driven experiences. And I'm building EveryLearner.ai to give K-6 teachers an AI-powered partner for differentiation, because no teacher should have to choose between personalization and sanity.

I came to educational technology through education itself, not the other way around. That shapes everything I do. I build tools that respect teachers, engage learners, and never forget that the point isn't the technology, it's the transformation.

My Journey

Career Milestones

2025

EveryLearner.AI

Founded EveryLearner.AI, an AI-powered lesson planning tool designed to give K-12 teachers an intelligent partner for differentiation—because no teacher should have to choose between personalization and sanity.

2024

RoleplayEd & SparkNC Partnership

Created RoleplayEd, an AI-powered immersive training environment with applications across education, healthcare, defense, and commercial sectors. Through our partnership with SparkNC, we helped develop their platform which won first prize for Impact at the 2024 Education Innovation Showcase at ASU+GSV Summit.

2021

UltiSim Education & QuestSim

Joined UltiSim Education as COO and Chief Learning Officer. The pandemic revealed how urgently education needed to change to give every student access to high-quality, engaging learning experiences—no matter where they live. Began designing and building QuestSim, our 3D simulation adventure learning platform delivered via browser.

2015-2021

Educational Leadership

Served as Assistant Headmaster & Head of Upper School at Buckley Country Day School (NY), then Director of Middle School at Canterbury School (NC). Led divisions, mentored teachers, implemented school improvement plans, and earned certificates in Advanced Educational Leadership and Leading Schools from Harvard.

2007-2013

Mathematics & Technology Education

Mathematics Teacher, Chair of Computer Science, and Director of IT at Cayman Prep and High School. Taught Algebra through Calculus, developed computer science curriculum, and designed educational technology practices for grades 7-12.

1999

The Beginning

Started my teaching career in Malawi, Central Africa as a 4th grade homeroom teacher at Phoenix International School. This began over 25 years of experience in education across four continents—Malawi, South Africa, the Cayman Islands, New York, and North Carolina.
