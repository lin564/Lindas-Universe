HIPAA Notice

This informational notice explains how HealthSimAI approaches HIPAA. A formal notice and Business Associate terms are provided as part of any deployment; contact info@healthsimai.com for details.

This website and PHI

This public marketing website does not request or process protected health information (PHI). Please do not include PHI in any form submission. Forms collect only business contact details.

How HealthSimAI handles PHI in deployments

Within contracted hospital deployments, HealthSimAI is designed to operate under a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with appropriate administrative, physical, and technical safeguards. Data sovereignty is a core principle — customer data stays within the customer's environment wherever possible.

Security heritage

HealthSimAI is built on UltiSim's mission-critical platform, with security practices forged in defense and federal environments.
