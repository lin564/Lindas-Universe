Privacy Policy

This is a plain-language summary of how HealthSimAI handles information submitted through this website. A complete policy is being finalized with counsel; contact info@healthsimai.com with any questions.

What we collect

When you submit a form on this site (a demo request, resource download, or readiness assessment) we collect the details you provide — typically your name, work email, organization, role, and any message — along with which resource you requested.

Why we collect it

We use this information to respond to your request, send the resources you ask for, understand which organizations are interested, and follow up about HealthSimAI. We do not sell your information.

Where it goes

Submissions are stored securely in our customer pipeline (operated within UltiSim's infrastructure) and are accessible only to authorized HealthSimAI team members.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your information at any time by emailing info@healthsimai.com.

This marketing site does not collect PHI

Please do not submit protected health information (PHI) through this website. Patient data is handled only inside contracted HealthSimAI deployments under appropriate agreements.
