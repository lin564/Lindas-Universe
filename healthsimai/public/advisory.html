Our advisory board pairs HealthSimAI with decades of health-system leadership across finance, operations, strategy, governance, and transformation, keeping our digital twins grounded in what hospitals truly need.

RB

Robert "Bob" Broadway

Healthcare Strategy, Finance & Health System Leadership

Bob Broadway is a senior healthcare executive with more than 40 years of leadership experience spanning healthcare finance, strategy, operations, business development, and governance. He served as VP and Chief Strategic Officer at Bethesda Health and as a Senior Advisor to Coble Health Group, and today leads Strategic Advisory HC, providing strategic counsel to healthcare organizations. A Fellow of the Healthcare Financial Management Association (FHFMA) and recipient of HFMA's Morgan Lifetime Achievement Award, Bob brings HealthSimAI deep health-system expertise and an executive-level understanding of the financial and operational decisions that drive healthcare transformation.

JT

Jeff Tarte

Healthcare Technology, Strategy & Public Sector Leadership

Jeff Tarte is a nationally recognized healthcare technology executive, management consultant, legislator, and strategic advisor. He served as CIO of Johns Hopkins Health System and New England Medical Center–Tufts University, was a healthcare IT partner at Ernst & Young, and is CEO of Cribb Philbeck, a management consulting firm. Jeff also served three terms in the North Carolina Senate and three terms as Mayor of Cornelius. A former CIO magazine Top 100 U.S. IT Executive and North Carolina Public Leader of the Year, Jeff brings HealthSimAI exceptional expertise in healthcare technology, enterprise strategy, government, and complex organizational transformation.

SA

M. Scott Armstrong

Healthcare Growth, Technology & Enterprise Partnerships

M. Scott Armstrong is a senior healthcare executive with 30+ years of leadership across healthcare services, technology, and managed services organizations. Most recently, as SVP of Strategic Partnerships at OptumInsight (UnitedHealth Group), he led large-scale enterprise revenue-cycle engagements with the nation's largest health systems. Previously, he served as President of OptumInsight's Southeast Region, SVP/Principal at HCA's Parallon, and held leadership roles with Allscripts, Siemens Healthineers, and Shared Medical Systems. Scott brings HealthSimAI deep C-suite relationships, extensive P&L and revenue leadership, and a proven record of building enterprise partnerships that deliver measurable financial and operational results.
