Our Mission

Hospitals shouldn't have to run blind.

Every day, healthcare leaders make high-stakes calls about beds, staff, operating rooms, and emergencies, all inside a live environment where mistakes cost lives and margins. HealthSimAI exists to take the guesswork out. Our AI digital twins let hospitals test decisions virtually, rehearse clinically, and act with confidence.

The road ahead is ambitious: partnering with 40 hospitals by the end of 2027 and scaling to 340+ by 2031, building a platform with a $1.2 billion annual revenue opportunity that's designed to return 10x to every hospital that deploys it, save the US healthcare system billions in annual medical expense, and above all, help save 100,000 lives a year.

We didn't start from zero. HealthSimAI is a venture of UltiSim Inc., whose mission-critical simulation technology has been trusted for decades by the U.S. military and government in the world's most demanding environments. That platform is the foundation under every hospital twin we build.

One twin. Two modes.

Every department twin we deploy does double duty: engineering your operations and training your people on the same living model.

Mode 01

Operations Optimization

Through systems engineering: test layouts, staffing, and capacity decisions in the twin before anything changes on the floor.

Mode 02

Clinical Training

For key personnel: surgical teams, triage protocols, and critical-event response rehearse inside the twin of their own department.
