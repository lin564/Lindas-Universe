Project Highlights

QuestSim

Co-Founder & Chief Learning Officer

QuestSim is a narrative-based 3D learning adventure platform that transforms how students engage with educational content. Our unique learning environments allow students to safely practice essential career skills, from professional communication to critical thinking.

The platform offers immersive simulation-based learning experiences that dramatically increase student engagement while building durable skills like problem solving, resilience, and creativity. Research shows simulation learning provides a 2-sigma improvement in understanding compared to conventional teaching methods.

3D Learning Simulation K-12 Education EdTech Platform
Visit QuestSim

Odysseus: The Hero's Journey

Product Lead

A 3D adventure learning game that immerses students in the world between the pages of Homer's "The Odyssey." Students don't just read about Odysseus's journey—they live it, making decisions, facing challenges, and experiencing the epic narrative firsthand.

Designed for English and Social Studies classrooms, this game makes ancient literature accessible and engaging for modern students while building reading comprehension, critical thinking, and narrative analysis skills.

English/ELA Social Studies Game-Based Learning Literature

Team Lab

Learning Design Lead

An interactive, persona-based game designed to teach students essential social skills and prepare them for the workforce. Students navigate realistic workplace scenarios, learning to collaborate, communicate, and resolve conflicts effectively.

Team Lab bridges the gap between academic learning and real-world professional success, helping students develop the soft skills that employers consistently rank as most important.

Social-Emotional Learning Workforce Readiness Collaboration Skills Simulation

EveryLearner.AI

Founder

An AI-powered lesson planning tool designed specifically for K-12 teachers. EveryLearner.AI gives teachers an intelligent partner for differentiation, because no teacher should have to choose between personalization and sanity.

The platform helps teachers create personalized learning experiences for every student in their classroom, dramatically reducing planning time while improving the quality and accessibility of instruction.

AI/Machine Learning Teacher Tools Differentiation K-12
