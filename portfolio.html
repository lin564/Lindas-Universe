Odysseus: The Hero's Journey

Product Lead

A 3D adventure learning game that immerses students in the world between the pages of Homer's "The Odyssey." Students don't just read about Odysseus's journey—they live it, making decisions, facing challenges, and experiencing the epic narrative firsthand.

Designed for English and Social Studies classrooms, this game makes ancient literature accessible and engaging for modern students while building reading comprehension and critical thinking skills.
